About SenseCEFR

SenseCEFR is an open-access, context-aware vocabulary profiler engineered to solve the Polysemy Flatline problem in CEFR language analysis by disambiguating multi-tier word senses in context.

License: MIT License

Datasets
  • CEFR-Annotated WordNet - Sense-level proficiency dataset (star092304)
  • Princeton WordNet & Morphy - Lexical database and morphological exceptions
  • CEFR-J 1.5 - Tokyo Univ. of Foreign Studies A1–B2 framework
  • CEFR Framework Standards - Council of Europe proficiency descriptors
  • Mihalcea WSD Corpora - Senseval/SemEval benchmarks & sense data
  • Octanove C1/C2 Profiles - Advanced open language profiles dataset
  • Oxford 3000™ / 5000™ - Graded wordlists, guidewords & collocations
